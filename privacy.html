Privacy

This is a personal portfolio. It doesn’t have accounts, forms, or ads, and I don’t run analytics or tracking scripts on it. Here’s everything it does with your data, in plain terms.

What the site stores

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it next time. That’s the only thing saved, it stays on your device, and it never reaches me. The site sets no cookies.

Third parties

  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, it handles requests and may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser, and pages requested, for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface, so your browser requests the font files from Google.

Email

The “Copy email” button only copies my address to your clipboard. If you email me, I’ll use your message just to reply, and I won’t share or sell it.
