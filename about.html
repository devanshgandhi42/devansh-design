About

I’m Devansh “Dev” Gandhi, a product designer in Seattle. For 5+ years I’ve designed consumer and growth products, at Toast now and before that at Duolingo, DoorDash, and Meta. I like taking complicated things and making them feel simple, whether that’s a daily learning habit, signing in to the right account, or a restaurant setting up its first ticketed event.

Experience

  • Toast, Product Designer, Consumer (Sep 2025 to now). I lead design for Toast Tables, Toast’s reservations and waitlist product, across the guest app, web, and restaurant tools. I designed and launched Ticketing for restaurant events, from event setup to guest checkout and door check-in, plus Notify (alerts when sold-out tables open up) and Live Activities for the waitlist.
  • DoorDash, Product Designer (contract), Growth (Jan to Aug 2025). I led Account Switching, which made multi-account login easier, and supported the Affordability team with concepts like Surprise Bags.
  • Duolingo, Product Designer (Jan 2022 to Nov 2024). I worked across Learning R&D and New Subject Areas, and designed and launched Daily Refresh and delight moments on the learning path.
  • Meta, Product Design Intern (summers 2019 and 2020). I designed Icebreakers for businesses on Messenger and Lightweight Reactions for Messenger Rooms.

Education

University of Washington, Bachelor of Design in Interaction Design (2017 to 2021), Annual Dean’s List.

What I care about

Education, small businesses, and immigration reform. I also mentor students and early-career designers, mostly on portfolios and breaking into product design.
